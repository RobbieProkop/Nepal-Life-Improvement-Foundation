Three redesign directions

Each link below is a complete, working single-page build using the real copy and the real photography. The Journal / Ascent combined build is now the live homepage; the three original explorations remain here for comparison.

A + C combined

Journal / Ascent, with a theme toggle

One page carrying both designs. Light mode is Field Journal — paper ground, Fraunces headlines, an inline portrait beside the headline, editorial prose with a drop cap, and the projects as a ruled ledger. Dark mode is Ascent — a full-bleed mountain photograph, Space Grotesk, topographic contours, the story pinned as scrolling chapters, and the projects as a horizontal traverse. The sun/moon button in the masthead switches between them.

Original explorations

Direction A

Field Journal

Documentary and editorial. Hairline rules and typographic hierarchy carry the layout instead of cards, shadows or rounded corners. Warm paper ground, ink text, a single marigold accent, and monospace for anything that behaves like metadata. Photographs get real captions and credits. The project history is a ruled ledger rather than a card grid.

Type
Fraunces display, DM Sans body, IBM Plex Mono labels
Strength
Distinctive while still reading as credible and institutional
Watch for
Leans quiet — less immediately arresting than B
View Field Journal

Direction B

Prayer Flag Riso

A screen-printed zine. Flat colour fields drawn from Buddhist prayer flags, duotone photographs, halftone and grain overlays, and hard offset borders in place of soft shadows. Type heavy enough to carry a poster. Elements are pinned up at angles and straighten when you reach for them. Nothing is rounded and nothing is blurred.

Type
Anton display, DM Sans body, Space Mono labels
Strength
The most memorable of the three; warm and handmade
Watch for
Least institutional — may cost you cautious major donors
View Prayer Flag Riso

Direction C

Ascent

Low-saturation and cinematic, built around the idea of a climb. The story is told as chapters whose photographs pin in place while the text scrolls past them, and the project history is a horizontal traverse. Topographic contour lines are generated in CSS rather than loaded as images. A reading-progress rail sits under the nav.

Type
Space Grotesk display and body, IBM Plex Mono labels
Strength
Best at telling the 2015-to-now story as a single arc
Watch for
Dark palette suits landscapes more than portraits
View Ascent

Every build shares one foundation: a modern reset, a dependency-free photo lightbox on the native <dialog> element, and a small script for the navigation, scroll reveals and progress rail. jQuery and lightbox2 are gone. In the combined build, all colour, type and texture decisions live in a single file, css/theme.css, which holds both palettes and maps them onto one set of semantic custom properties. For comparison, the current site is at ../index.html.
